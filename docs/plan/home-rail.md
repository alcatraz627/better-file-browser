# Home, rail layout: what each part is for

The owner picked the rail layout on 28 Oct (decision page bfb-home-layout-3).
They asked for numbers that are useful and can be clicked, icons in the path
bar, and links to Settings and Help. This page says what each part is for.

## What Home is for

Home is where a new tab lands. So do the toolbar icon, `g h`, and closing the
last tab. At that moment the owner wants one of four things:

1. **Go somewhere.** Type a path, or pick a place used before.
2. **See what waits on them.** Decisions to answer, asks in directions docs.
3. **Pick up where they left off.** A chat, a note, a place opened lately.
4. **Glance at how things are going.** Light numbers, never a report.

Every part below serves one of these. Nothing is there to fill space.

## The parts

**Head.** A greeting and the date sit on the left. On the right is a row of
icon buttons. They start work from nowhere: New note, Ask, Open from Finder,
Command palette, Keyboard map and Settings. Each tooltip names its key. These
are the "other links" the owner asked for, kept small and where the eye starts.

**Path bar.** It runs full width under the head. Suggestions come from the
folder being typed and from recent places. Its buttons are icons: open here,
open as a web link, and choose from Finder. The `l` key puts the cursor here.

**Numbers.** Six tiles. Each answers a question the owner might ask, and each
is a button that goes to the answer.

| Tile | Shows | Click |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting on you | decision pages waiting, last two weeks | jumps to Decisions |
| Asks on you | asks in directions docs, and how many repos | jumps to Directions |
| Questions | questions asked this week, 7 small bars | jumps to Continue |
| Places opened | places opened today, 7 small bars | opens the History lens |
| Saved | places saved, and tags | opens the Saved lens |
| Notes | notes, and how many changed this week | opens the Notes lens |

The bars show the last seven days, today on the right. Hover one for its
count. Counts that need the owner take the accent colour when above zero.

**Main column.** Decisions and Directions sit side by side. Each scrolls
inside its card past about ten rows. Continue sits below them at full width,
because a chat row carries a line of its answer.

**Rail.** Saved, Notes and Opened lately stack in a fixed column on the right.
Each row has its menu or its forget button.

## What changed from the first version

- The greeting card and the numbers took 40% of the first screen. The head is
  now one compact band.
- Every unseen decision was tinted gold, so the list read as one block. Unseen
  is now a dot and a bold title.
- The numbers were trivia, such as decisions answered. They are now things the
  owner acts on, and each one goes somewhere.
- The path bar's text buttons are icons, and Settings and Help are one click.
